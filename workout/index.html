45-Minute Dumbbell Prescription Plan

Precise Biomechanical Protocols & Execution Standards • HonWeng 🦊

Phase 0: Yoga Mobility (5 Mins)

1. Child's Pose (Balasana)

1 Min Continuous
🎯 Target: Lumbar Decompression, Lat Stretch, Diaphragmatic Breath
Setup & Position Knees wide (mat-width), big toes touching. Sink hips back directly onto heels. Walk hands forward until arms are fully elongated.
Prescriptive Execution Rest forehead on mat. Actively press palms into floor to push hips deeper onto heels. Creep fingertips forward 1 inch every 20 seconds.
Breathing Pattern 4-second inhale expanding the lower ribs and lower back 360°; 6-second slow nasal exhale letting chest sink towards the floor.
Common Faults to Avoid Lifting hips off heels; shrugging shoulders up into ears; shallow chest-only breathing.

2. Cat-Cow Segmented Flow

1 Min (10 Full Cycles)
🎯 Target: Segmental Spinal Mobility & Pelvic Tilt Coordination
Setup & Position Quadruped position: Wrists directly under shoulders, knees directly under hips. Neutral neck, fingers spread wide for wrist support.
Prescriptive Execution Cow: Inhale, tilt pelvis anteriorly, drop belly, pull chest through shoulders, look slightly forward.
Cat: Exhale, tuck tailbone first, dome upper back toward ceiling, press floor away with palms.
Tempo & Cadence 3 seconds Cow (inhale) → 1 second pause → 3 seconds Cat (exhale) → 1 second hold at peak spinal flexion.
Common Faults to Avoid Moving solely from the neck/lower back rather than articulating each individual vertebra sequentially.

3. Thread the Needle

1 Min (30s Left / 30s Right)
🎯 Target: Thoracic Spine Rotation & Rear Shoulder Mobility
Setup & Position All-fours stance. Keep pelvis level and knees anchored evenly into the mat.
Prescriptive Execution Reach right arm under chest, sliding back of hand along the floor until right shoulder and temple rest gently on the mat. Press left palm into floor to gently leverage thoracic rotation.
Breathing Pattern Inhale to fill the back of the lungs; exhale to sink 2mm deeper into the twist.
Common Faults to Avoid Shifting hips sideways; collapsing neck weight without shoulder support.

4. Downward Dog with Pedaling Feet

1 Min Continuous
🎯 Target: Posterior Chain, Gastroc/Soleus Calves, Serratus Anterior
Setup & Position Hands shoulder-width, feet hip-width. Form an inverted "V" shape by driving hips up and back.
Prescriptive Execution Press actively through index knuckles to protect wrists. Alternate bending one knee while driving the opposite heel firmly toward the floor (hold each heel down 3 seconds).
Breathing Pattern Slow rhythmic breaths, drawing lower belly inward on each exhale.
Common Faults to Avoid Rounding the lower back (bend knees slightly if hamstrings are tight to preserve flat lumbar spine).

5. World's Greatest Stretch (Low Lunge + T-Spine Rotation)

1 Min (30s / side)
🎯 Target: Hip Flexors, Adductors, Thoracic Spine & Chest Expansion
Setup & Position Deep runner's lunge with left foot forward outside left hand. Back leg straight or knee lightly down.
Prescriptive Execution Drop left elbow inside left instep for 2 seconds. Then rotate left arm straight up to the ceiling, following fingertips with your eyes. Switch sides at 30s.
Breathing Pattern Exhale as you rotate open towards the ceiling; inhale as you return down to instep.
Common Faults to Avoid Letting the front knee flare wildly out of control; overarching lower spine instead of twisting mid-back.
Phase 1: Lower Body Power (12 Mins)

Goblet Squat

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Quadriceps, Gluteus Maximus, Anterior Core Bracing
Setup & Stance Feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, toes flared 15–30°. Hold one dumbbell vertically with palms cupping upper bell against mid-sternum. Elbows pointing down.
Prescriptive Execution Initiate by breaking at hips and knees simultaneously. Drive knees out in line with second toes. Descend until hip crease is parallel to knee caps. Drive floor away through whole foot.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3 seconds controlled descent → 1 second pause in the hole (no bouncing) → 1 second explosive drive up → 0 second rest at top.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Deep belly inhale & brace before descent; sharp exhale past sticking point on way up.
Avoid: Knees caving inward (valgus); heels lifting; dumbbell drifting away from chest.

Dumbbell Reverse Lunges

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps/Leg • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Unilateral Quad Strength, Glute Medius Stability, Ankle Control
Setup & Grip Stand tall, feet hip-width apart. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with neutral grip, arms fully extended at sides. Retract shoulder blades slightly.
Prescriptive Execution Step back 2.5 to 3 feet with left leg. Lower hips vertically until both front and rear knees reach 90° angles (rear knee hovering 1 inch off floor). Drive through front heel to return.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 2 seconds back/down → 1 second hold at 90° → 1 second drive to standing. Complete all reps on one leg before switching.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale stepping back; exhale driving through front heel to stand.
Avoid: Front knee shooting forward past toes; torso leaning excessively forward; slamming rear knee into floor.

Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Hamstrings (Eccentric Loading), Glute-Hamstring Tie-in, Spinal Erectors
Setup & Grip Feet hip-width apart, toes pointing straight forward. Overhand grip holding dumbbells across front of thighs. Shoulders pulled down and locked into back pockets.
Prescriptive Execution Maintain a 15° soft bend in knees (knees stay fixed). Push hips straight back towards the wall behind you. Let weights trace down along the front of shins until mid-shin / hamstring stretch. Squeeze glutes to stand.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3 seconds slow hinge back → 1 second isometric hamstring stretch at bottom → 1 second glute contraction back to vertical.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale at top, brace core through hinge, exhale locking hips out.
Avoid: Rounding lumbar spine; squatting down by bending knees further; letting dumbbells drift forward away from legs.
Phase 2: Upper Body Push (10 Mins)

Standing Overhead Press

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Anterior & Lateral Deltoids, Clavicular Pectoralis, Core Stability
Setup & Stance Feet hip-width, glutes squeezed tight, abs braced. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with elbows angled 30° forward in the scapular plane (not flared 90°).
Prescriptive Execution Press straight overhead in a slight arc until biceps are aligned with ears at full lockout. Lower dumbbells under strict control back to collarbone level.
Prescribed Tempo 2-0-1-0: 1 second vertical press → 0 second pause → 2 seconds controlled lowering to shoulders.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale before pressing; exhale through top half of press.
Avoid: Leaning back into lumbar hyperextension; flaring elbows wide; using leg bounce (keep it strict).

Dumbbell Floor Press

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Pectoralis Major, Anterior Deltoids, Triceps Brachii
Setup & Position Lie flat on back on the mat. Bend knees to 90° with feet flat on floor. Hold dumbbells with elbows at a 45° arrow angle relative to torso.
Prescriptive Execution Press dumbbells upward until arms are straight, focusing on squeezing chest at top. Lower slowly until back of upper arms lightly touch the floor (zero bounce).
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3 seconds lowering → 1 second dead stop on floor (deloads shoulders) → 1 second explosive chest drive.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale lowering dumbbells; exhale forcefully on chest press.
Avoid: Flaring elbows out to 90° (T-shape); bouncing triceps violently off hard floor; arching lower back excessively.

Bent-Over Tricep Kickbacks

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Triceps Brachii (Peak Contraction of Long and Lateral Heads)
Setup & Stance Hinge torso forward to 45° with flat back. Pull elbows up and pin upper arms parallel to your ribcage.
Prescriptive Execution Keeping upper arms 100% stationary, extend forearms backward until arms are completely locked out straight. Squeeze triceps hard for 1 full second at the apex.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1 second extension → 1 second hard isometric squeeze → 2 seconds return to 90° elbow bend.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale bending elbows; exhale extending arm back.
Avoid: Swinging elbows forward like a pendulum; rounding back; using excessive weight that limits full lockout.
Phase 3: Upper Body Pull (10 Mins)

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps/Arm • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids, Middle Trapezius, Biceps
Setup & Stance Staggered stance (or one hand on bench/chair for support). Torso angled at 45° with a flat, rigid spine. Hold dumbbell with arm hanging straight down.
Prescriptive Execution Initiate by driving elbow backward along an arc toward your hip pocket (not straight up to chest). Retract shoulder blade at top. Lower with full stretch at bottom.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1 second pull to hip → 1 second isometric squeeze of lat → 2 seconds lowering to full lat stretch.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale at full bottom extension; exhale while driving elbow back.
Avoid: Rotating torso or jerking shoulder to cheat weight up; pulling dumbbell into armpit using only biceps.

Standing Dumbbell Bicep Curls

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Biceps Brachii (Short/Long Heads), Brachialis, Grip Strength
Setup & Stance Stand with feet hip-width. Squeeze glutes to eliminate body swing. Dumbbells at sides, palms facing forward (or starting neutral, supinating as you curl).
Prescriptive Execution Pin elbows against ribcage. Curl weights up without letting elbows drift forward. Squeeze biceps forcefully at peak height (wrist aligned with front delt).
Prescribed Tempo 3-0-1-0: 1 second curl up → 0 second pause → 3 seconds strict negative descent.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Exhale curling up; inhale slowly lowering down.
Avoid: Rocking torso backward; allowing elbows to flare out or swing forward to take tension off biceps.

Bent-Over Reverse Flys

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Posterior Deltoids, Rhomboids, Infraspinatus (Posture Fix)
Setup & Grip Use light dumbbells. Hinge forward at hips until torso is nearly parallel to floor. Maintain flat back. Arms hang down with elbows slightly unlocked (micro-bend).
Prescriptive Execution Lead with elbows and sweep arms out wide to the sides in a reverse hugging motion until weights are level with shoulders. Pinch upper shoulder blades together.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1 second lateral raise → 1 second hold at peak contraction → 2 seconds controlled return.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Exhale sweeping arms out wide; inhale returning down.
Avoid: Shrugging upper traps; using momentum to bounce weights; straightening arms into rigid lockout.
Phase 4: Finisher & Decompression (8 Mins)

Dumbbell Thrusters (Conditioning Finisher)

2 Min AMRAP (As Many Reps As Possible)
🎯 Target: Full-Body Power, Kinetic Leg-to-Arm Energy Transfer, VO2 Max
Setup & Position Racked position: Dumbbells resting on front deltoids with elbows high. Feet shoulder-width apart.
Prescriptive Execution Drop into full squat depth. Drive explosively out of the bottom with leg power, transferring momentum directly into a fluid overhead dumbbell press.
Prescribed Rhythm Fluid continuous cycle. Catch dumbbells at shoulders as you simultaneously descend into the next squat.
Common Faults to Avoid Pausing at shoulders between squat and press (make it one continuous movement); pressing before hips have fully extended.

Decompression Static Cool-Down

6 Mins Total (45–60s Per Hold)
🎯 Target: Heart Rate Recovery, Fascial Lengthening, Parasympathetic State
1. Pigeon Pose (Glutes/Piriformis) Right shin forward across mat, left leg extended straight back. Lower chest down toward mat. Hold 60s per side.
2. Doorway / Wall Chest Stretch Forearm against wall at 90°, step forward with same-side foot and rotate torso away to stretch pec minor and anterior delt. 45s per side.
3. Seated Single-Leg Hamstring Fold Extend one leg straight, hinge at hips reaching for toes with flat back. Hold 45s per leg.
Breathing Protocol Deep 4-second nasal inhale, 6-second relaxed exhale through mouth. Target bringing heart rate below 100 BPM.
