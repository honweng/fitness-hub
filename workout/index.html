45-Minute Dumbbell Protocols

Prescriptive Biomechanical Strength & Hypertrophy Plans • HonWeng 🦊

Phase 0: Yoga Mobility (5 Mins)

1. Child's Pose (Balasana)

1 Min Continuous
🎯 Target: Lumbar Decompression, Hip Flexor Release, Diaphragmatic Breath
Setup & Position Knees wide (mat-width), big toes touching. Sink hips back directly onto heels. Walk hands forward until arms are fully elongated.
Prescriptive Execution Rest forehead on mat. Actively press palms into floor to push hips deeper onto heels. Creep fingertips forward 1 inch every 20 seconds.
Breathing Pattern 4-second inhale expanding lower ribs and lower back 360°; 6-second slow nasal exhale letting chest sink.
Common Faults to Avoid Lifting hips off heels; shrugging shoulders into ears; shallow chest-only breathing.

2. Cat-Cow Segmented Flow

1 Min (10 Full Cycles)
🎯 Target: Segmental Spinal Mobility & Pelvic Tilt Coordination
Setup & Position Quadruped position: Wrists under shoulders, knees under hips. Neutral neck, fingers spread wide for wrist support.
Prescriptive Execution Cow: Inhale, tilt pelvis forward, drop belly, pull chest through shoulders.
Cat: Exhale, tuck tailbone first, dome upper back toward ceiling, push floor away.
Tempo & Cadence 3 seconds Cow (inhale) → 1 second pause → 3 seconds Cat (exhale) → 1 second hold at peak spinal flexion.
Common Faults to Avoid Moving solely from neck/lower back rather than articulating each vertebra sequentially.

3. Thread the Needle

1 Min (30s Left / 30s Right)
🎯 Target: Thoracic Spine Rotation & Rear Shoulder Mobility
Setup & Position All-fours stance. Keep pelvis level and knees anchored evenly into the mat.
Prescriptive Execution Reach right arm under chest, sliding back of hand along floor until right shoulder and temple rest on mat. Press left palm into floor to leverage thoracic rotation.
Breathing Pattern Inhale to fill the back of the lungs; exhale to sink 2mm deeper into the twist.
Common Faults to Avoid Shifting hips sideways; collapsing neck weight without shoulder support.

4. Downward Dog with Pedaling Feet

1 Min Continuous
🎯 Target: Posterior Chain, Gastroc/Soleus Calves, Serratus Anterior
Setup & Position Hands shoulder-width, feet hip-width. Form an inverted "V" shape by driving hips up and back.
Prescriptive Execution Press through index knuckles. Alternate bending one knee while driving opposite heel down (hold each heel down 3 seconds).
Breathing Pattern Slow rhythmic breaths, drawing lower belly inward on each exhale.
Common Faults to Avoid Rounding the lower back (keep knees slightly bent if hamstrings are tight).

5. World's Greatest Stretch

1 Min (30s / side)
🎯 Target: Hip Flexors, Adductors, Thoracic Spine & Chest Expansion
Setup & Position Deep runner's lunge with left foot forward outside left hand. Back leg straight or knee lightly down.
Prescriptive Execution Drop inside elbow to instep for 2 seconds. Rotate arm straight up to the ceiling, following fingertips with eyes.
Breathing Pattern Exhale as you rotate open towards the ceiling; inhale as you return down to instep.
Common Faults to Avoid Front knee collapsing inward; overarching lower spine.
Phase 1: Lower Body Power (12 Mins)

Goblet Squat

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Quadriceps, Gluteus Maximus, Anterior Core Bracing
Setup & Stance Feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, toes flared 15–30°. Hold one dumbbell vertically cupped at sternum.
Prescriptive Execution Break at hips and knees simultaneously. Drive knees out in line with second toes. Descend to parallel. Drive floor away through whole foot.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3s descent → 1s pause in the hole (no bouncing) → 1s explosive drive up.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale into belly & brace before descent; sharp exhale driving up.
Avoid: Knee valgus (caving inward); heels lifting; weight drifting off chest.

Dumbbell Reverse Lunges

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps/Leg • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Unilateral Quad Strength, Glute Medius Stability, Ankle Control
Setup & Grip Stand tall, dumbbells at sides with neutral grip. Retract shoulder blades slightly.
Prescriptive Execution Step back 2.5–3 feet. Lower hips vertically until both knees reach 90° (rear knee hovering 1 inch off floor). Drive through front heel to return.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 2s back/down → 1s hold at 90° → 1s drive to standing.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale stepping back; exhale driving through front heel to stand.
Avoid: Front knee shooting forward past toes; excessive forward torso lean.

Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Hamstrings (Eccentric Loading), Glute-Hamstring Tie-in, Spinal Erectors
Setup & Grip Feet hip-width, toes forward. Overhand grip with dumbbells across thighs. Lats locked tight.
Prescriptive Execution Maintain 15° soft knee bend (fixed). Push hips straight back towards wall. Skim weights down shins until deep hamstring stretch. Squeeze glutes to stand.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3s slow hinge back → 1s hamstring stretch hold at bottom → 1s glute drive to lockout.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale & brace at top; exhale locking hips out.
Avoid: Rounding lumbar spine; squatting down by bending knees further.
Phase 2: Upper Body Push (10 Mins)

Standing Overhead Press

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Anterior & Lateral Deltoids, Clavicular Pectoralis, Core Stability
Setup & Stance Feet hip-width, glutes clenched, abs braced. Dumbbells at shoulder level with elbows angled 30° forward in scapular plane.
Prescriptive Execution Press vertically until arms lock out with biceps beside ears. Lower under strict control to collarbones.
Prescribed Tempo 2-0-1-0: 1s vertical press → 0s top pause → 2s controlled descent.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale before pressing; exhale through the push.
Avoid: Hyperextending lower back (leaning back); flaring elbows 90° out to sides.

Dumbbell Floor Press

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Pectoralis Major, Anterior Deltoids, Triceps Brachii
Setup & Position Lie flat on back with knees bent and feet flat. Hold dumbbells with elbows at a 45° arrow angle to torso.
Prescriptive Execution Press dumbbells straight up, squeezing chest at top. Lower slowly until back of upper arms lightly touch the floor (zero bounce).
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3s lowering → 1s dead stop on floor (deloads shoulders) → 1s explosive press.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale lowering down; forceful exhale on chest press.
Avoid: Elbows flaring out into a "T" position; bouncing triceps off the floor.

Bent-Over Tricep Kickbacks

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Triceps Brachii (Peak Contraction of Long and Lateral Heads)
Setup & Stance Hinge torso to 45° with flat spine. Pin elbows to ribcage and keep upper arms parallel to floor.
Prescriptive Execution Keeping upper arms completely stationary, extend forearms back until arms are locked straight. Squeeze triceps hard for 1 second.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1s extension → 1s hard contraction squeeze → 2s return to 90°.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale bending elbow; exhale extending backward.
Avoid: Swinging elbows forward like a pendulum; rounding back.
Phase 3: Upper Body Pull (10 Mins)

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps/Arm • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids, Middle Trapezius, Biceps
Setup & Stance Staggered stance (or one hand supported on bench/chair), torso at 45° with flat spine.
Prescriptive Execution Drive elbow back along an arc toward your hip pocket (not vertical to armpit). Retract shoulder blade at top, then lower to full lat stretch.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1s pull to hip → 1s lat squeeze at apex → 2s lowering to full stretch.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale at full bottom extension; exhale driving elbow back.
Avoid: Twisting torso or yanking with shoulder momentum; pulling only with biceps.

Standing Dumbbell Bicep Curls

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Biceps Brachii (Short/Long Heads), Brachialis, Grip Strength
Setup & Stance Stand tall, feet hip-width, glutes engaged. Dumbbells at sides, palms facing forward.
Prescriptive Execution Pin elbows against ribcage. Curl dumbbells up without letting elbows drift forward. Squeeze biceps hard at top.
Prescribed Tempo 3-0-1-0: 1s curl up → 0s pause → 3s strict negative descent.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Exhale curling up; inhale slowly lowering down.
Avoid: Rocking torso; letting elbows swing forward to cheat tension off biceps.

Bent-Over Reverse Flys

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Posterior Deltoids, Rhomboids, Infraspinatus (Posture Fix)
Setup & Grip Light weight. Hinge at hips until torso is nearly parallel to floor. Micro-bend in elbows.
Prescriptive Execution Sweep arms wide to sides in a reverse hugging motion until level with shoulders. Pinch upper shoulder blades together.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1s raise → 1s hold at peak contraction → 2s controlled lowering.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Exhale sweeping arms wide; inhale returning down.
Avoid: Shrugging neck traps; swinging weights with momentum.
Phase 4: Finisher & Decompression (8 Mins)

Dumbbell Thrusters (AMRAP Finisher)

2 Min AMRAP (As Many Reps As Possible)
🎯 Target: Full-Body Power, Kinetic Leg-to-Arm Energy Transfer, VO2 Max
Setup & Position Racked position: Dumbbells resting on front deltoids with elbows high. Feet shoulder-width apart.
Prescriptive Execution Drop into full squat depth. Drive explosively out of bottom with leg power, transferring momentum directly into an overhead dumbbell press.
Prescribed Rhythm Fluid continuous cycle. Catch dumbbells at shoulders as you simultaneously descend into next squat.
Common Faults to Avoid Pausing at shoulders between squat and press; pressing before hips have fully extended.

Decompression Static Cool-Down

6 Mins Total (45–60s Per Hold)
🎯 Target: Heart Rate Recovery, Fascial Lengthening, Parasympathetic State
1. Pigeon Pose (Glutes/Piriformis) Right shin forward across mat, left leg extended straight back. Lower chest down toward mat. Hold 60s per side.
2. Doorway / Wall Chest Stretch Forearm against wall at 90°, step forward with same-side foot and rotate torso away. 45s per side.
3. Seated Single-Leg Hamstring Fold Extend one leg straight, hinge at hips reaching for toes with flat back. Hold 45s per leg.
Breathing Protocol Deep 4-second nasal inhale, 6-second relaxed exhale. Target bringing heart rate below 100 BPM.
Phase 0: Yoga Mobility (5 Mins)

Proven Yoga Mobility Flow

5 Mins Continuous Flow
🎯 Target: Whole-Body Joint Fluidity, Hip & Thoracic Prep
1. Child's Pose (1 Min) Hips back to heels, deep lower back diaphragmatic breathing.
2. Cat-Cow Segmented Flow (1 Min) Slow vertebral articulation and pelvic tilt awareness.
3. Thread the Needle (1 Min) Thoracic spine twist and posterior shoulder opening (30s/side).
4. Downward Dog + World's Greatest (2 Mins) Calf pedaling into deep runner's lunge rotational twists.
Phase 1: Lower Body Variations (12 Mins)

Dumbbell Sumo Squats

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Adductor Magnus (Inner Thighs), Gluteus Maximus, Pelvic Floor
Setup & Stance Wide stance (1.5x shoulder width), toes flared out 45°. Hold one heavy dumbbell vertically hanging straight down between legs.
Prescriptive Execution Push knees actively outward over toes as you lower hips straight down. Keep torso noticeably more upright than standard squats. Squeeze glutes and adductors hard at the top.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3s controlled descent → 1s pause in deep stretch → 1s explosive ascent.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale lowering down; exhale driving through heels.
Avoid: Knees collapsing inward; leaning forward with the chest.

Goblet Squats (1.5 Rep Style)

3 Sets × 8–10 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Maximum Quadriceps Time-Under-Tension & Knee Stability
Setup & Grip Standard shoulder-width stance. Hold dumbbell tight against sternum with elbows pointing down.
Prescriptive Execution Descend to full squat depth → come up halfway → drop back down to full depth → drive all the way to standing. That equals 1 complete repetition!
Prescribed Rhythm Continuous tension. Do not lock out or rest at the halfway bounce point.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Deep inhale at top; short exhale on final lockout.
Avoid: Using momentum on the half-rep; rounding upper back.

Romanian Deadlifts (RDL Anchor)

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Hamstrings (Eccentric Stretch), Glute-Ham Tie-in, Lower Back
Setup & Grip Feet hip-width, dumbbells skimming front thighs. Shoulders retracted and depressed.
Prescriptive Execution Hinge at hips pushing glutes backward while maintaining a fixed soft knee bend. Feel the intense hamstring stretch at mid-shin level before standing tall.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3s slow lowering → 1s isometric hold at bottom → 1s glute contraction up.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale at top; brace through hinge; exhale at standing lockout.
Avoid: Rounding lumbar spine; letting weights drift away from legs.
Phase 2: 3D Rotational Push (10 Mins)

Seated / Standing Arnold Press

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Complete 3D Deltoid Head Recruitment (Front, Lateral, Rear)
Setup & Stance Hold dumbbells at chin height with palms facing your chest, elbows tucked tight in front of body.
Prescriptive Execution In one fluid motion, spread elbows out to sides while rotating palms forward 180° as you press vertically overhead. Reverse smoothly on the way down.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-2-0: 2s smooth rotation and press → 1s top lockout → 2s controlled return to chin.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Exhale pressing and rotating up; inhale returning to chest.
Avoid: Jerking weights; arching lower back; disconnecting the rotation from the press.

Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Floor Press

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Triceps Brachii, Clavicular Pectoralis, Rotator Cuff Relief
Setup & Position Lie flat on mat, knees bent at 90°. Hold dumbbells with palms facing each other (neutral grip).
Prescriptive Execution Tuck elbows at 45° close to ribcage. Lower until triceps gently touch floor, pause for 1s dead stop, then drive through chest and triceps to press straight up.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3s lowering → 1s floor pause → 1s explosive drive.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale on descent; sharp exhale pressing up.
Avoid: Bouncing elbows off hard floor; flaring elbows out wide.

Overhead Dumbbell French Press

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Long Head of Triceps (Upper Arm Mass & Overhead Stability)
Setup & Grip Stand tall (or sit). Cup both palms under the top plate of a single heavy dumbbell overhead.
Prescriptive Execution Keeping upper arms locked vertically beside ears, bend elbows to lower weight behind head until deep triceps stretch, then extend forearms back to lockout.
Prescribed Tempo 3-1-1-0: 3s slow descent behind head → 1s deep stretch → 1s extension.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale lowering behind neck; exhale pressing overhead.
Avoid: Flaring elbows wide apart; moving upper arms back and forth.
Phase 3: Upper Back & Grip Pull (10 Mins)

Dual Bent-Over Dumbbell Rows (Overhand Grip)

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 60s
🎯 Target: Upper Lats, Rhomboids, Middle Trapezius, Core Anti-Flexion
Setup & Stance Hinge forward at 45° with flat back. Hold both dumbbells with overhand (pronated) grip, knuckles facing forward.
Prescriptive Execution Row both dumbbells simultaneously, pulling elbows wide and high toward lower ribcage. Squeeze shoulder blades together forcefully at the apex.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1s pull → 1s hard scapular squeeze → 2s lowering to full stretch.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Inhale at bottom; exhale while rowing weights up.
Avoid: Rounding lumbar spine; standing upright using momentum.

Dumbbell Neutral-Grip Hammer Curls

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Brachialis Muscle (Pushes Bicep Peak Higher), Forearm Brachioradialis
Setup & Stance Stand tall, feet hip-width. Hold dumbbells at sides with palms facing inward towards thighs (thumbs pointing up).
Prescriptive Execution Pin elbows to sides. Curl dumbbells straight up while maintaining neutral grip throughout. Squeeze hard at top without letting elbows swing forward.
Prescribed Tempo 3-0-1-0: 1s curl up → 0s pause → 3s strict eccentric descent.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Exhale curling up; inhale slowly lowering down.
Avoid: Torso sway; swinging elbows forward.

Bent-Over Reverse Flys (Posture Anchor)

3 Sets × 10–12 Reps • Rest: 45s
🎯 Target: Posterior Deltoids, Rhomboids, Scapular Retractors
Setup & Grip Light dumbbells. Hinge forward at 45–60° with flat back. Micro-bend in elbows.
Prescriptive Execution Sweep arms wide to sides in a reverse hugging motion until level with shoulders. Pinch upper shoulder blades together.
Prescribed Tempo 2-1-1-0: 1s raise → 1s hold at peak contraction → 2s controlled lowering.
Breathing & Faults Breath: Exhale sweeping arms wide; inhale returning down.
Avoid: Shrugging neck traps; swinging weights with momentum.
